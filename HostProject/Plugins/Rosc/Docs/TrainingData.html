The AI models' training data

Each model learnt from sets of pictures that others made and own. The licence of a model's weights does not say whether the terms of that data reach what you make with the model, and the law has not settled it. Some of the owners publish their data for research or other non-commercial use only. If paid work depends on a model, read the terms of its data first, or ask a lawyer. This table is what the models' makers and the owners publish, as found on 26 November 2026; it is not legal advice.

Below each model: who holds the licence of its weights, then the data it learnt from. A name links to the page where the owner states the terms.
